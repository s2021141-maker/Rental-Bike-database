The homepage layout is intended to look something like this, a very minimalistic style interface that doesn't overwhelm users with many buttons on the screens but at the same time also give them the information needed, as well as enough interactive features being easily accessible with icons such as the profile on the top right and the exit button on the top right being represented as symbols to simplify their overall design while also being very easily understandable to new users.

This is not the final look, something I believe should be implemented is a more colorful user interface with blue, white, etc to make the website look less bland and more user-friendly.
